From the record

Two months in the record: May–June 2026

Between 2025-01-01 and 2026-06-13, JEM entered 1828 incidents into the hate incident tracker. This page reads that record — where documentation concentrated, what kinds of incidents it holds, and what is known about the legal response. Every figure is computed from the published dataset.

1828 records · 2025-01-01 – 2026-06-13 · Source: JEM Hate Incident Tracker · dataset of 2026-08-04

The map accompanies the text

When this page is served over the web, a live map of the 1631 geolocated records moves alongside each section. Every figure and every cited record is in the text itself — nothing here is available only by map.

01 · The record

1828 incidents, entered and sourced

The JEM hate incident tracker is a register of documented hate incidents against religious minorities in India. Each entry carries a date, a place, a category, the legal status as reported, and its sources. Over May and June 2026 the register took in 1828 records across 51 states and union territories, with 89 recorded as online incidents.

One caution before any figure: this is a record, not a census. It contains what could be documented and verified — an undercount by construction. Where a detail could not be corroborated, the entry says so.

Source: JEM Hate Incident Tracker dataset (CC BY 4.0), 1828 records, 2025-01-01 – 2026-06-13.

03 · Religious spaces

162 attacks on religious spaces — many by official order

The record holds 162 attacks on religious spaces in these two months, 58 of them in Uttar Pradesh. A distinct pattern runs through the June entries: demolition and sealing drives against mosques and madrasas, carried out by local administrations citing land, regulatory or road-widening grounds. 12 of the dated entries in this category fall between 2026-06-09 and 2026-06-13.

2026-06-09 · Near Rajghat, Varanasi · Uttar Pradesh · Attacks on religious spaces A mosque reported to be nearly 200 years old was demolished in a late-night operation under heavy security; authorities stated the structure stood on railway land. Read the full record on the tracker → 2026-05-12 · Moradabad · Uttar Pradesh · Attacks on religious spaces A development authority team, with police deployment, used bulldozers in a demolition drive against a madrasa near the National Highway. 04 · The largest concentration

Uttar Pradesh: 540 records, 30% of the register

No state appears in the record as often as Uttar Pradesh — 540 of the 1828 incidents, more than the next three states combined. The entries span every category in the register: acts of hate (152), acts of violence (85), attacks on religious spaces (58), and cases of discrimination and exclusion (110).

2026-05-12 · Dahra village, Hapur district · Uttar Pradesh · Act of violence A group reportedly entered a Muslim family’s house during a procession day, vandalised the property and vehicles and assaulted family members; no FIR or police action is confirmed in the record. Read the full record on the tracker → 2026-05-11 · (Derwa area)Pratapgarh district, Uttar Pradesh · Act of violence An elderly resident returning home from a walk was reportedly stopped, abused on account of his faith and beaten; an FIR was reportedly registered. Read the full record on the tracker →

05 · Police atrocity

116 records where the police are the reported actor

In 116 entries across 15 states, the reported harm came from the police themselves — custodial assault, or arrest and action directed at members of a minority where no offence by them is clearly on record. These cases sit apart in the register because the institution a victim would ordinarily turn to is the one named in the report.

2026-05-08 · Vejalpur, Ahmedabad · Gujarat · Police atrocity A Muslim man taken into custody on allegations related to cow slaughter was reportedly assaulted by police personnel, with footage of the incident circulating. Read the full record on the tracker → 2026-06-05 · Muzaffarnagar · Uttar Pradesh · Police atrocity A food blogger was arrested after objections to a promotional video whose opening frames showed a city landmark; police cited complaints of hurting religious sentiments, with the exact legal provisions unspecified. Read the full record on the tracker →

06 · State action

176 records of state-sponsored discriminatory practice

A further 176 entries record administrative action reported to single out a minority community — demolition and eviction notices, cancelled permissions, restrictions on worship. Several cluster in the days before Eid al-Adha in early June. Together with the demolitions filed under attacks on religious spaces, they form the register's clearest through-line: harm arriving on official letterhead.

2026-06-04 · Tiljala–Topsia area, Kolkata · West Bengal · State-sponsored discriminatory practice Residents of a Kolkata neighbourhood received civic notices declaring their buildings unauthorised and giving 48 hours to vacate, days before Eid al-Adha. Read the full record on the tracker → 2026-05-15 · Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) · Maharashtra · State-sponsored discriminatory practice A municipal corporation conducted an early-morning anti-encroachment drive with significant police deployment, reportedly targeting structures linked to an elected minority representative. Read the full record on the tracker →

07 · The accountability picture

What the record says about legal response

401
records mention an FIR
137
no confirmed official action
692
carry 2+ source links

Only 401 of the 1828 records mention a First Information Report in their reported legal status, and in 137 records the legal-status field notes that no confirmed official action — no statement, arrest or proceeding — had been reported at the time of entry. The field records what is reported, not an adjudication.

On sourcing: 692 records carry two or more source links, and 1631 of the 1828 are geolocated to locality precision — pins mark localities, never addresses.

Computed from the legal_status and sources fields of the dataset. Accused persons are presumed innocent unless convicted by a court.

08 · What the record is for

A record exists to be cited

These 1828 entries are published to be used: cited in reporting and research, reused under CC BY 4.0 with attribution, and corrected on the record when an error is found. The tracker carries every entry behind this page — with its sources, its legal status and its reference.
